Property information

Water, gas & electricity

Water is supplied by Thames Water. You cannot choose your water supplier.

Gas and electricity: you can choose your supplier. Compare deals regularly. Power cut: call 105.

Broadband

Availability depends on your block or address. Check Hyperoptic, BT Openreach and other providers for fibre options.

Council tax & bins

Council tax is paid to Reading Borough Council. Band depends on your property.

Bins and recycling are collected by Reading Borough Council. Large items collection is available for a fee. Re3 recycling centre is nearby.

Door entry & fobs

Most blocks use electronic entry systems. Contact the managing agent for replacement or additional fobs (proof of address required).
