Local Information

Kenavon Drive is a residential estate in Reading, close to the River Kennet, with shared gardens, riverside path access and good links to the town centre.

Nearby: Reading Station (short bus or walk), shops, cafés, and the Re3 recycling centre.

Bus services connect to Reading Station. Check Reading Buses for current routes and times.
