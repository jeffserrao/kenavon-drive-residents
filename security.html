Security & communal areas

If you see something that is not right, report it. Call Police on 101 (non-emergency) or 999 (emergency). You can also report to the managing agent during office hours.

Do not allow unknown people into secure areas after you. Do not leave doors unlocked.

Private CCTV: film only your own property. Do not record neighbours' gardens or public spaces without permission. External cameras on the front of buildings may breach covenants. Check with the managing agent.

Bin and bike stores have changing codes. Contact the managing agent with proof of address for the latest code.
