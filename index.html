About the estate

Kenavon Drive is a mixed residential development close to the River Kennet, with shared gardens, podium spaces and riverside path access. It includes apartments and houses across several blocks, with communal areas managed by the RMC.

Good links to Reading town centre and station. Local amenities, parks and recycling facilities are nearby.

RMC & managing agent

The RMC is led by resident directors. Day-to-day management, repairs, cleaning, landscaping and service charge queries are handled by the appointed managing agent (Trinity Estates / FirstPort).

All residents can raise issues through the managing agent. Decisions on major works and budgets are made by the RMC directors.
