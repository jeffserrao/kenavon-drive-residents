Welcome

This is the residents portal for Kenavon Drive estate in Reading. Managed by the Kenavon Drive (Reading) Management Company Ltd (RMC).

Use this site for practical information on utilities, moving in, security, local facilities and how to contact the managing agent for repairs and service charges.

Quick Links

Report a repair or fault: Contact the managing agent (Trinity Estates / FirstPort) via their resident portal or phone.

Service charges: View your account and payments through the managing agent's online system.

Fobs and entry codes: Request replacements or current bin/bike store codes from the managing agent with proof of address.

Emergency: Police 999 (or 101 non-emergency). Power cut: 105. Gas emergency: 0800 111 999.

About the Estate

Kenavon Drive is a mixed residential development in Reading, close to the River Kennet, with shared gardens, podium spaces and riverside path access. It includes apartments and houses across several blocks, with communal areas managed by the RMC.

The estate benefits from good links to Reading town centre and station. Local amenities, parks and recycling facilities are nearby.

RMC & Managing Agent

The RMC is led by resident directors. Day-to-day management, repairs, cleaning, landscaping and service charge queries are handled by the appointed managing agent (Trinity Estates / FirstPort).

All residents can raise issues through the managing agent. Decisions on major works and budgets are made by the RMC directors.

Company number: 09414035.

Key Information

Water: Thames Water (you cannot choose your supplier).

Gas & Electricity: You can choose your supplier. Compare deals regularly.

Broadband: Check Hyperoptic, BT Openreach and others for your specific block or house.

Bins & recycling: Reading Borough Council collections. Large items collection available for a fee. Re3 recycling centre nearby.

Council tax: Paid to Reading Borough Council.
