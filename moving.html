Moving in / out

  1. Take gas, water and electricity meter readings and send to your suppliers.
  2. Set up broadband as early as possible.
  3. Contact Reading Borough Council for council tax and register to vote.
  4. Update your address with banks, doctors, dentist and other organisations.
  5. Consider Royal Mail redirection for post.

If renting, complete a check-in/out inspection and consider an end-of-tenancy clean. Do not place furniture in bin stores (fly-tipping is illegal). Use council collection or Re3 for large items.
